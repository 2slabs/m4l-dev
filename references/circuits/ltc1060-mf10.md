## LTC1060 / MF10 section (m4l_dev/circuit_modules/ltc1060_mf10_section.py)

**Linear Technology, LTC1060 datasheet, rev. "1060fb"** (`sources/ltc1060-1060fb.pdf`; a university mirror,
https://www.komponenten.es.aau.dk/fileadmin/komponenten/Data_Sheet/Switch_Cap/LTC1060.pdf, sha256 4114b99d...;
official copy https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/1060fb.pdf).
- p. 1: "Operates Up to 30kHz" (the module's highest centre); "pin compatible with MF10"; p. 2: "Clock Feedthrough, fCLK ≤ 1MHz: 10 mV(P-P)"; the clock-to-center ratio
  tested at 50:1 and 100:1.
- p. 7 (pin 12, "50/100/Hold"): "By tying Pin 12 to (V +A and V +D), the filter operates in the 50:1 mode"; "By
  tying Pin 12 to 1/2 supplies (which should be the AGND potential), the LTC1060 operates in the 100:1 mode" (the
  module's `clock_ratio`, a strap); "When Pin 12 is shorted to the negative supply pin, the filter operation is stopped and the bandpass
  and lowpass outputs act as a S/H circuit holding the last sample. The hold step is 20mV and the droop rate is
  150µV/second!"; "With low supply operation, the maximum input clock frequency is about 500kHz. Beyond this, the
  device exhibits its excessive Q enhancement and center frequency errors."
- p. 8: "The LTC1060 is a sampled data filter and it only approximates continuous time filters"; "From Graph 4 at
  ±7.5V supply, 50:1 and 1.4MHz clock, a Q of 5 has about 8% error"; "Mode 3 ... and the modes of operation where R4
  is finite, are 'slower' than the basic mode 1 ... The finite GBW of this op amp creates an additional phase shift
  and enhances the Q value at high clock frequencies."

**Texas Instruments (National), MF10-N datasheet, SNOS547C (2013)** (`sources/mf10-n.pdf`,
https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/mf10-n.pdf, sha256 0e837564...).
- p. 1: "Operation up to 30 kHz"; p. 3: "Center Frequency Range", max 30 kHz.
- p. 4: "Clock Feedthrough, Q = 10, Mode 1: 10 mV"; voltage swing ±3.8 V min, ±4.25 V typ (N/AP/HP, ±5 V).
- p. 26, "Sampled data system considerations": an input above half the clock "will be 'reflected' to a frequency
  less than one-half the sampling frequency"; "the output signal changes amplitude once every sampling period,
  resulting in 'steps' in the output voltage which occur at the clock rate"; "A ratio of 100:1 will reduce any
  aliasing problems ... a ratio of 50:1 ... will result in 3 dB lower output noise."

**Which chip, which ratio** (the device that asked for it, 2sl filter: its references/sherman-filterbank.md §2.0):
the Sherman Filterbank's designer, in a 2025 interview, "the clock is ratio 100 to one".

What the curves say: no Q error up to a knee, then a steep rise, sooner for higher Q (mode 3, ±5 V, 100:1: Q 50 at
0.67 MHz, Q 5 at 0.97 MHz) and sooner at lower supplies and in mode 3 than mode 1. In damping (q = 1/Q) every family
is close to ONE offset whatever the Q: the excess phase of op amps that settle less and less within a clock period.

The worst families are Graph 4's and the ±2.5 V mode 3 ones, where a Q 5 curve bends (its drafted line has a kink)
or the high-Q curve is cut short by its label. Against the text's one number (p. 8: "From Graph 4 at ±7.5V supply,
50:1 and 1.4MHz clock, a Q of 5 has about 8% error") the 50:1 ±7.5 V fit gives 5.9 % and Graph 4's drawn line 11 %.

The module (`Q_ENHANCEMENT_FITS`, the table above) picks the fit by the character's `chip_mode` and `supply_volts` and
the section's `clock_ratio`; default mode 3 at ±5 V, the bold row: mode 3 because it is the configuration whose pins
are the module's (p. 13, "Figure 11. Mode 3: 2nd Order Filter Providing Highpass, Bandpass, Lowpass"), ±5 V because
the character's swing (`full_scale_volts`) is the ±5 V part's. **Above a family's highest drawn clock the law is
extrapolated** (the default's: 1.405 MHz, a 14 kHz centre at 100:1; at 2.2 MHz, 22 kHz, it gives dq = 0.17).

**Which mode and supply the Sherman Filterbank's chips run in is UNVERIFIED**: no schematic or measured unit. Mode 3 at
100:1 is a reading (100:1 from the designer, R§2.0 of 2sl filter's research; mode 3 from the outputs the device mixes),
held as a character setting, not a fact about the hardware.

## The module

The module is our construction from these: the two-integrator section stepped per tick with LDI integrators (forward
and backward Euler in the loop, the standard discrete form of a switched-capacitor biquad, whose poles sit at
F = 2 sin(pi f0 / f_clock); not stated in either datasheet), Q enhancement as the fitted law above, the 10 mV p-p
feedthrough against the MF10's ±4 V swing as full scale, hold as stopped ticks. The band-limiting is the kit's
band-limited edges technique (../dsp.md), chosen by measurement (module docstring).
The character (`Ltc1060Mf10Character`) holds what differs from unit to unit: the op amps' swing (`full_scale_volts`,
4 V: between the MF10's ±3.8 V min and ±4.25 V typ), the rail knee, the clock feedthrough, and the chip's mode and
supply (which pick its Q enhancement).
