# Patterns and variation (topic 3, primary)

Format: 16 steps = one bar of 16ths, step 1 = beat 1, steps 1/5/9/13 = beats, 3/7/11/15 = 8th offbeats. `X` accent, `x` hit, `.` rest. Swing convention (Attack, https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/passing-notes/daw-drum-machine-swing/): 16th-note swing, 50% = straight, applied to every second 16th.
Voices: BD SD RS CP CH OH RD (ride) CR (crash) LT/MT/HT.
Honest limit: Beat Dissected / Studio Brootle grids are screenshots, not text. Text-verifiable placements only are transcribed; `?` = the source says the voice exists but gives no placement in text. No source found with a real Deepchord/Echospace drum grid (see Gaps). No accent data exists in any genre source below (all say velocity-flat or give no accents), so everything is `x` unless stated.

## A. Transcribed patterns

### P1 Basic-Channel-style dub techno (Attack) - the closest sourced dub techno grid
- Tempo 145 BPM, swing NONE (50%), 909-ish samples + Ableton Drum Buss. https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/beat-dissected/basic-channel-style-dub-techno/
- Placement read from the article's Step-2 MIDI screenshot (one image read): kick on every beat, snare+clap on beats 2 and 4, open hat on every 8th offbeat. Text says "snare on every other downbeat" and "four-to-the-floor kick and off-beat hat" (same URL).
```
BD  x...x...x...x...
SD  ....x.......x...
CP  ....x.......x...   (lo-fi clap layer doubling the snare: bit-crushed, EQ low cut 150 Hz)
OH  ..x...x...x...x.
```
- Percussion layer: "a couple hits exactly on offbeat 16th notes"; groove comes "from delay instead of heavily swung drum parts" (same URL). Sub bass riff = two pitched sub hits, ducked by kick; no grid in text.
- Delay (not swing) is the groove source: 1/8 dotted echo on percussion loop, 5/16 echoes on chord layer (same URL).

### P2 Detroit techno, 909 samples (Attack) - rim/snare counterpoint
- 120-125 BPM, swing 55-60%. https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/beat-dissected/motor-city-detroit-techno/
```
BD  x...x...x...x...   "four-to-the-floor with no variation"
SD  ?   RS  ?          "rimshot and snare patterns work against each other"; "rimshot doesn't ever hit on the same beat as a kick"
CH1 ?   CH2 ?          two closed hats, hat 1 sharp/bright, hat 2 soft slower attack, "very subtle velocity" on hat 2
OH  ..x...x...x...x.   "every off beat"
```
- No velocity variation on kick/snare/rim; "interplay of the parts" provides dynamics. Hats can be muted individually per arrangement section; one mute group (closed cuts open) is standard, separate groups let open tails ring on "for a looser, rougher effect" (same URL).

### P6 Rolling techno (Attack)
- 131 BPM, listed "Swing 20%" (convention unclear - see Disagreements). https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/beat-dissected/rolling-techno/
- Ghost kick "before each beats two and four"; three filtered toms alternating on every 16th; claps on the 16th grid across two bars with low-velocity ghost hits; offbeat open hat on "second and fourth quarter" with second one "pushed slightly back in time", velocity ~50%, a few removed "to create more variation"; off-beat hat plus fast 16th hat, LFO-on-delay-time to move hats in time.

```
TOM xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx  (alternating three toms, every 16th)
CP  ghosts around main hits (grid in screenshot only)
OH  ..x...x...x...x.  (some removed bar to bar)
```

- https://beatkitchen.io/guides/electronic-music/03-drum-programming/ . Text states: clap steps 5 and 13, closed hats on every off-beat 8th, open hats on the "and" of beats 2 and 4, swing 52-55%.

### P9 Jeff Mills-style rolling 909 (MusicRadar) - 137 BPM, different style but the only 909-variation recipe from MusicRadar
- Kick on each beat, offbeat open hat between kicks; open-hat Release and Transpose mapped to controllers and recorded over 16 bars; second 16-bar section gets different automation; ride triggered with the same notes as the open hat, -6 dB, sidechained to kick. https://www.musicradar.com/how-to/how-to-program-jeff-mills-style-rolling-tr-909-drums
```
BD  x...x...x...x...
OH  ..x...x...x...x.
RD  ..x...x...x...x.  (duplicate of OH notes, -6 dB)
```

### P10 Techno short-loop trick (Studio Brootle)
- "Use shorter loops like 3 or 5 1/16 notes and then duplicate them across the bar for a rolling feel"; velocity can modulate filter cutoff, volume and note length so alternating ride hits are more filtered and shorter (on a 909 this is accent = volume only). 137 BPM base. https://www.studiobrootle.com/techno-drum-patterns-and-drum-programming-tips/
```
HAT-3  x..x..x..x..x..x   (3-step loop repeated, wraps across the bar)
HAT-5  x....x....x....x
```
(the two strings are my literal expansion of "loop of 3 / 5 sixteenths"; the source gives no grid.)

### P11 Euclidean reference rhythms (Toussaint)
- Source: https://cgm.cs.mcgill.ca/~godfried/publications/banff.pdf (mirrored). E(k,n) = k onsets spread as evenly as possible over n steps.
```
E(3,8)  x..x..x.          Cuban tresillo
E(5,8)  x.xx.xx.          cinquillo
E(5,16) x..x..x..x..x....  bossa-nova necklace (usually started on 3rd onset: x..x..x...x..x..)
E(4,12) x..x..x..x..
E(2,5)  x.x..   E(3,4) x.xx   E(3,7) x.x.x..   E(5,12) x..x.x..x.x.
```
- Toussaint also writes the 2-bar son clave as `x..x..x...x.x...`. Same PDF.
- (4 on the floor = E(4,16) trivially; not stated in the paper.)

## B. How patterns are varied (sourced mechanisms)

### B1 Grids: density threshold + randomness perturbation (code, most directly reusable)
Source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pichenettes/eurorack/master/grids/pattern_generator.cc
- Each node stores a per-step level (0-255) per channel; a step fires when `level > ~density[channel]` (density knob = threshold; low density keeps only the strongest steps).
- At the start of each pattern (step 0) a per-channel perturbation = random byte * (randomness>>2)/256 is drawn and ADDED to every level of that channel for the whole pattern pass (clipped at 255). So randomness makes weak steps fire in whole-pattern variants, not independent coin flips.
- Accent: a firing step with level > 192 sets the accent bit (separate accent outputs).
- Swing mode: randomness knob is reconfigured as swing: `value = randomness * 43 >> 8` (pulses), added to steps with !(step & 2), subtracted otherwise; randomness set to 0 in that mode.
- Euclidean mode: per channel length (1-32) and density (0-31) index a lookup table of bit patterns (`lut_res_euclidean`), steps refreshed on 16ths only.
- Docs: X/Y map position, 3 density knobs, randomness knob, accent outputs; Euclidean mode as hidden option. https://pichenettes.github.io/mutable-instruments-documentation/modules/grids/

### B2 Elektron conditional trigs / probability / fill (official manual)
Source: https://www.elektron.se/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Digitakt-User-Manual_ENG_OS1.54_260930.pdf , sec 10.9.3-10.9.5 (mirrored).
- X% = "x% chance that the trig will be active"; replaces PROB with COND on the trig page.
- FILL / NOT FILL: active only when FILL mode on / off. FILL mode: one pattern cycle starting at next loop, or while key held, or latched.
- A:B: true on pass A, then every B passes. 1:2 = passes 1,3,5...; 2:2 = 2,4,6...; 2:4 = 2,6,10...; 4:7 = 4,11,18...
- PRE / NOT PRE: true if the most recently evaluated condition on the same track was true (conditionless trigs not counted); NEI / NOT NEI: same on the previous track; 1ST / NOT 1ST: first pass only.
- Swing: 51-80%, default 50%. Micro timing per trig. Manual suggests "mutually exclusive" note trigs plus probability locks "to make the most minimal of compositions come alive".
- Fill-every-8-bars recipe (forum, anecdotal): 64-step pattern, fill trigs conditioned 2:2, non-fill trigs 1:2; or NOT PRE chains. https://www.elektronauts.com/t/drum-fills-on-8th-bar/193320

### B3 Ableton Live 12 MIDI Tools (official manual)
Source: https://www.ableton.com/en/live-manual/12/midi-tools/ (mirrored).
- Rhythm: Steps (max 16), Pattern (shape; count depends on Steps and Density), Density (number of notes, max = Steps), Split (probability a step is divided in half), Shift (rotate by steps), Velocity + Accent, Accent Frequency (notes between accents), Accent Offset. Per drum pad; run again on another pad to layer.
- Seed: random notes within pitch/length/velocity ranges; Density = % of range populated.
- Euclidean (Max for Live): up to 4 voices, per-voice Rotation, randomise-rotation button, Steps, Density (repeats within selection), Division.

### B4 The TR-909's own variation tools (owner's manual, mirrored)
Source: https://archive.org/details/synthmanual-roland-tr-909-owners-manual (text mirrored via mirror_patterns.sh; no genre example patterns in the manual - only blank grids).
- Steps per measure 1-16 (Last Step), Scale button changes steps per beat; 4 tracks x 2 banks; pattern groups.
- Accent A "Total Accent": all voices on that step; Accent B per-voice, only BD, SD, LT, MT, HT, CH; accent intensity per voice cannot be changed, Total Accent has a knob; if Total Accent knob is high, voice accent "may not be noticed".
- Shuffle: only with 16th (and triplet) scale; 7 levels at 16th scale (1 = none, faster bounce toward 7); 4 distinct levels at the other scale (1=2, 3=4, 5=6, 7). Odd measure count: last step gets no bounce.
- Flam: BD, SD, LT, MT, HT only; 8 intervals (keys 9-16), longer toward 16; independent of tempo knob; first hit is on the step, second follows.
- Open/closed hat share a voice (press both main keys of the hat voice for open), so a closed hit chokes the open hat.
- Hardware spec: 48 patterns (16 x 3 groups) x 2 banks; 1-16 steps. https://support.roland.com/hc/en-us/articles/201921899-TR-909-Technical-Specifications

### B5 Producer practice (numbers as given; secondary)
- Quantize kick/snare ~100%, hats/perc 50-75%; start at 60-70% for most genres. Hat velocity: beats 110-127, offbeats 70-90, in-between 40-60; random velocity offset 10-20%; ghost notes vel 15-35 ("techno rarely uses them"); kick push 5-10 ticks early; snare drag 5-10 ticks late; hat timing drift 3-8 ticks; swap an open hat for a closed hat every 8 bars; change one hat velocity / move one ghost per copy. https://beatkitchen.io/guides/electronic-music/03-drum-programming/
- Techno swing 0-54% (Attack P5: 50-54%); house 52-56% (Beat Kitchen) vs 50-60% (Attack deep house); lo-fi/hip-hop 55-60% (Beat Kitchen).
- Rimshots "a staple in reggae and dub rhythms"; processed with tape-style delay: rim 7/32 repeat, regen 100%, "Gallop" pattern; hats 303 ms echo, feedback 35%; crash 2/16T, feedback 85%; 140 BPM demo. https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/tutorials/dub-style-drum-processing-for-character-and-groove/
- Mute/arrangement variation: three hats can be muted individually between sections (Attack Detroit, P2); percussion mirrors the hat so they enter/leave separately (P3); automate open-hat release/transpose over 16 bars and different automation in the next 16 (P9); automate hat decay tail short-then-long (P5).
- Hat length variation for groove: cut sampler release to 0 so audio length = drawn note length, vary hat note lengths. https://www.studiobrootle.com/techno-drum-patterns-and-drum-programming-tips/
- Rolling variation: remove a few offbeat open hats and push the second one late at ~50% velocity (P6).

## C. Disagreements and unclear
- Rolling techno "Swing 20%" vs Attack's stated convention 50% = straight (swing article, deep house comments): a 20% value is not valid on that scale; treat as unknown/different unit. https://www.attackmagazine.com/technique/beat-dissected/rolling-techno/
- Basic Channel text "snare on every other downbeat" vs screenshot (beats 2 and 4): used screenshot. 
- Swing for house: Beat Kitchen 52-56% vs Attack 50-60% / 55-60%; dub techno specifically: Attack says no swing, groove via delay.
- Beat Kitchen says hats "every off-beat 8th" while the Attack Detroit piece uses OPEN hat on offbeats and CLOSED hats interplaying elsewhere: hat open/closed assignment on offbeats differs by source.
- Elektron swing range 51-80% vs 909 shuffle 7 discrete levels: different models of the same idea; no source maps 909 level N to a percent.
